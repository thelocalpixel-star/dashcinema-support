Start here

Connection check

  1. Connect your iPhone to the vehicle and open DashCinema on the car display.
  2. Confirm you’re safely parked. Car video requires a compatible iPhone and a vehicle that supports Apple’s CarPlay video feature; audio-only CarPlay is not enough.
  3. On iPhone, open Cast, start Screen Broadcast and choose DashCinema in Apple’s prompt.
  4. Keep the iPhone unlocked while checking the connection. Stop sharing using iPhone’s recording indicator when you’re finished.

Still blank?

Try Cast → Run picture check. If the test picture works but a particular video does not, the source may block capture or external playback.

Stop and restart the broadcast, then reconnect CarPlay while parked. An unsupported vehicle cannot be unlocked by changing a setting or purchasing a plan.

Common questions

A few things worth knowing.

Why is Netflix or another streaming app blank?

Netflix does not support screen mirroring. Other services may block recording or external playback for some or all titles, causing a blank picture, missing sound or stopped playback. DashCinema cannot bypass DRM. A working test picture is not proof that a streaming service permits sharing.

Does every CarPlay vehicle support video?

No. DashCinema’s App Store candidate uses Apple’s system video route. Your iPhone, iOS version and vehicle must support it, and Apple and the vehicle control availability. A car that supports CarPlay audio does not necessarily support video. Video is for safely parked use only.

How do I add my own videos or media server?

Add authorized videos from Files, direct video links or M3U playlists. More → Media servers supports Plex, Jellyfin and Emby connections. Server playback is direct-play only, so use files with a supported format. DashCinema does not supply channels, movies or third-party subscriptions.

What is planned for subscriptions?

The planned App Store offer is 30 minutes of total playback, then a choice of monthly or yearly Pro access. Live billing and the allowance are still being prepared. Current RevenueCat Test Store actions are tests, not real charges. The final price and billing period will appear before you confirm any real purchase. No purchase will make a protected app or unsupported car compatible.

What should I include in a support email?

Tell us your app version, iOS version, vehicle model and what you were doing. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, server tokens, private media links, VINs or screen recordings containing personal information.
